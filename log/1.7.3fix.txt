# 1.7.3fix

## 中文

- 修复安装车万女仆时，部分方法注入失败导致游戏无法启动的问题。
- 首次自然驯服女仆时自动记录契约，即使当时未戴潘多拉项链，之后戴上也能获得爱恋，仍永久绑定第一位女仆，每人一次。待领记录在死亡、重登后保留，获取说明同步更新。
- 为错过奖励且无绑定记录的旧存档玩家增加一次补领：佩戴潘多拉项链，潜行右键自己的女仆，领取爱恋并永久绑定她。已领取、已有绑定或违誓永久损毁后不可补领。
- 修复先接收转让女仆，再首次自然驯服时无法获得爱恋的问题。
- 修复进化宝珠与其他飞行来源交替时，飞行权限错误残留或被收回的问题。
- 修复塑魂、强壮属性的生命加成在读档时丢血，以及加成降低或消失后生命超过上限的问题。
- 修复反转 HUD 在小窗口下，过长条目无法完整显示、滚动查看或点击的问题。
- 优化塑魂续期、反转界面请求同步和潘多拉诅咒附魔查询，减少重复处理；效果条件与动态附魔仍即时检测。

适用 Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.10+。建议客户端和服务端同时更新至 1.7.3fix。必装潘多拉之咒 2.4.26+、Curios 5.4.5+；若安装 JEI，需 15.20.0.106 或更新版本；车万女仆为可选联动，需 1.5.3 或更新版本。

## English

- Fixed method injection failures that prevented the game from starting with Touhou Little Maid installed.
- Your first naturally tamed maid is now recorded even without a Pandora Necklace. Wear one later to receive Love once per player, permanently bound to that same maid. Pending rewards survive death and relogging, and acquisition descriptions have been updated.
- Added a one-time recovery for older saves that missed the reward and have no recorded binding: wear a Pandora Necklace and sneak-right-click your own maid to receive Love and bind her permanently. Previous claims, existing bindings and permanent vow bans cannot be bypassed.
- Fixed receiving a transferred maid preventing Love from being awarded on the first natural tame.
- Fixed the evolved orb incorrectly retaining or revoking flight when other flight sources changed.
- Fixed health bonuses from Soul Sculpting and the Strong attribute losing current health on load, or leaving health above its maximum when reduced or removed.
- Fixed long reversal HUD entries becoming unreadable, impossible to scroll through or unclickable in small windows.
- Optimized Soul Sculpting renewal, reversal HUD request synchronization and Pandora Curse enchantment lookups to reduce repeated work. Effect conditions and dynamic enchantments are still checked immediately.

For Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.10+. Update both client and server to 1.7.3fix. Requires Curse of Pandora 2.4.26+ and Curios 5.4.5+. JEI is optional; if installed, use 15.20.0.106 or newer. Touhou Little Maid is optional; if installed, use 1.5.3 or newer.
